# MythCards soundtrack direction

Updated October 18, 2026, from Drew's direction.

The shared language is 8–16-bit game music with room for richer sounds. Favor
lo-fi downtempo grooves: warmth, restrained upper frequencies, space, rounded
bass, relaxed timing, and characterful synthetic instruments. Avoid piercing
lead tones. Low fidelity is a texture, not an excuse for clipping or excessive noise.

Area themes establish a place and its culture/lore. Preserve Closed City — Behind
the Gates v2 as the approved Closed City exploration/base theme for eventual
single-player play. Do not overwrite it when creating a character theme.

Leader themes express personality within the area's world and should feel more
luxurious. Irina's theme combines cultivated luxury, private access, and controlled
menace. Matches select the starting player's specific leader theme first, then
an available area base; unscored areas remain silent.

Use roughly 83-second main loops as the current reference. Eventually give leader
themes a second compatible loop featuring instrumental improvisation, interplay,
and a breakdown. Compose those as distinct arrangements with intentional entry
and exit points. Irina's second loop, After Hours, is now composed as a separate
listening draft. Automatic A/B transitions are future work, outside current playback.

The Threshold remains the approved shared menu and team-selection ambience.
