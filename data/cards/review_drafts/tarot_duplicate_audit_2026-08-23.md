# Tarot Duplicate Audit - 2026-08-23

Purpose: identify tarot archetypes mapped to more than one distinct card inside the same sub-area draft.

## Summary

- Far North has no distinct-card duplicate in the draft card list. Nadia Belaya appears twice because she is listed once as a known story character and once as a draft card.
- Winter Front has 6 duplicate tarot assignments across different cards.
- Global duplicates across different areas are expected because each physical area/sub-area can eventually become its own tarot deck.

## Winter Front Duplicates

### Page of Swords

- Radio Lieutenant / Lieutenant Pavel Orlov - Character / Specialist
- Radio Snow Speaks - Event
- Frozen Field Radio - Relic

Recommended fix:
- Keep Radio Lieutenant / Lieutenant Pavel Orlov as Page of Swords.
- Remap Radio Snow Speaks to Eight of Wands for urgent messages, signals, and transmissions crossing distance.
- Remap Frozen Field Radio to Ace of Swords for the first clear impossible signal/truth cutting through static.

### Seven of Cups

- Enemy Cache Unearthed - Event
- Enemy Occult Cache - Relic

### The Emperor

Recommended fix:

## Notes

These recommendations preserve the currently approved character mappings and move the duplicate events/relics into unused or better-fitting archetypes.
