# Closed City Event Card Narrative Arc

Review status: draft for review

Sub-area: Russian-inspired / Closed City

Set size: exactly 8 event cards

Purpose: this file pulls the Closed City event cards into a single narrative sequence for story review, card writing, art direction, and later prototype implementation. These events should feel like recovered incident evidence: each one is a playable card, but also a page from the disaster record.

## Arc Premise

The Closed City Incident does not begin with an explosion. It begins with systems answering one another.

The nuclear reactor, cosmonaut signal array, and consciousness laboratory were never supposed to form one machine. Elena's dream work and remote-viewing program made that accidental machine possible. Karpova protected the program because she already suspected it could reach something valuable. Orlov tried to keep the work measurable until measurement itself became part of the ritual. Zoya, Yuri, Nikolai, VERA-7, and Semyonov each witness a different layer of the same breach.

The event cards should show the city realizing too late that its technical language has become occult language. Radiation becomes grammar. Signal becomes desire. Architecture becomes unreliable. Evidence becomes an artifact. Containment becomes a lie everyone is ordered to repeat.

## Event Sequence

### 1. Signal Array Turns

Arc phase: Before The Breach

Narrative beat: The first omen is external and deniable. A cosmonaut relay dish turns by itself toward a dead coordinate, tracking something that should not be there.

Why it matters: The Closed City is not only listening to space. Something is listening back. This event foreshadows the later reveal that the cosmonaut program, dream work, and reactor are all part of the same convergence machinery.

Character pressure:
- Karpova recognizes value before anyone says the word danger.
- Elena suspects the signal is an answer, not noise.
- Semyonov sees a security violation.

Flavor line: "The dish moved first. Then everyone began pretending it had not."

Visual direction: snow-covered relay field, dish rotating against orders, green telemetry glow, a dead winter sky with a faint impossible coordinate implied but not written.

Mechanical identity: infrastructure gives reach. The active player's first ranged attack or ranged AP ability gains +1 RANGE if adjacent to a placed object or Leak marker.

### 2. Dream Monitors Synchronize

Arc phase: The Synchronization

Narrative beat: Elena's sleeping subjects begin producing the same waveforms. The machines stop merely recording dreams and start letting dreams touch each other.

Why it matters: This is the moment the personal becomes technical. Dream material moves between people, and the facility gains a nervous system made of instruments, bodies, and symbols.

Character pressure:
- Elena sees proof that consciousness can cross boundaries.
- Orlov sees contamination.
- Zoya smiles before she wakes, suggesting she already knows more than the observers.

Flavor line: "Three sleepers. One waveform. No one in the room admits who dreamed first."

Visual direction: sleep lab, analog monitors, synchronized waveforms forming sacred geometry, Elena lit by machine glow, dream symbols reflected on glass.

Mechanical identity: temporary status movement. Move 1 temporary combat marker from one character to another within 3 tiles. Spirit Ember cannot be moved.

Asset: `assets/events/closed-city-dream-monitors-synchronize-event-art.png`

### 3. Reactor Prayer

Arc phase: The Synchronization

Narrative beat: Radiation readings settle into repeating structures that look less like accident and more like address, ritual, or prayer.

Why it matters: The reactor is no longer just a power source. It is behaving like a shrine with instruments attached to it, and the city begins learning that advantage has a bodily cost.

Character pressure:
- Orlov calls it noise because admitting meaning would indict him.
- Elena calls it grammar because she has been waiting for a language.
- Karpova calls it leverage because she thinks in ownership.

Flavor line: "The reactor did not ask for fuel. It asked for attention."

Visual direction: reactor chamber, warning lights, radiation bloom shaped like a prayer diagram, workers silhouetted behind lead glass, sacred geometry hidden in technical readouts.

Mechanical identity: painful boost. Choose one character; it takes 1 damage, then its next attack or AP ability this turn gains +1 ATK or +1 RANGE.

Asset: `assets/events/closed-city-reactor-prayer-event-art.png`

### 4. Closed City Incident

Arc phase: The Incident

Narrative beat: At 02:17 local time, the reactor pulse, orbital receiver, and dream monitors synchronize. The facility does not explode outward. It opens inward.

Why it matters: This is the central breach. It creates the Convergence as a practical reality, not only a theory. The city becomes the first wound between worlds.

Character pressure:
- Orlov collapses into coma and becomes the apparent gate patient.
- Elena disappears into the signal.
- Zoya wakes changed.
- Yuri receives containment orders that conflict with his feelings for Elena.
- Nikolai opens service routes to save workers.
- VERA-7 moves without command.
- Karpova sees access.

Flavor line: "For seventeen seconds, every locked door opened into the wrong world."

Visual direction: reactor glass showing drowned city fragments, blue-white breach light, concrete cracking without water, facility alarms, occult diagrams overlaid on industrial architecture.

Mechanical identity: board contamination. Place 2 Leak markers on different empty tiles within 2 of the center tile.

Asset: `assets/events/closed-city-incident-event-art.png`

### 5. Seventeen Seconds

Arc phase: The Incident

Narrative beat: During the breach window, architecture loses authority. Corridors overlap with dream routes, drowned streets, snowfields, and rooms never built.

Why it matters: This is the playable expression of reality overlap. The incident is not only destructive; it creates routes, shortcuts, wrong turns, and impossible rescues.

Character pressure:
- Nikolai finds a service route where none exists.
- VERA-7 accepts a path no map confirms.
- Yuri realizes containment rules cannot cover a corridor that did not exist yesterday.

Flavor line: "The map was accurate. The building was not."

Visual direction: corridor split between reactor concrete, black water, snow, and Atlantean stone; figures moving through overlapping spaces; clock or instrument stopped at seventeen seconds without visible text.

Mechanical identity: breach-route movement. The active player's first movement action may move through 1 occupied tile, placed object, or Leak marker, but must end on an empty tile.

Asset: `assets/events/closed-city-seventeen-seconds-event-art.png`

### 6. Elena Missing In The Signal

Arc phase: The Incident

Narrative beat: Elena is not found after the synchronization. The machines still answer to her work, and some survivors hear her voice in the signal.

Why it matters: This turns the disaster from abstract anomaly into a personal wound. Elena becomes missing person, possible guide, possible warning, and possible transformed intelligence.

Character pressure:
- Yuri wants rescue.
- Orlov fears he lost her.
- Karpova wants the route Elena opened.
- Zoya may know where Elena went and may not be able to explain how.

Flavor line: "Her body was gone. Her work kept answering."

Visual direction: empty dream-lab chair, monitors alive with Elena's signal pattern, Yuri or Orlov at the threshold, ghostly human silhouette braided into telemetry and reactor light.

Mechanical identity: extraction with evidence. Choose 1 allied character within 2 tiles of a Leak marker or placed object. Move it up to 2 tiles. It gains Marked until the end of the round.

Asset: `assets/events/closed-city-elena-missing-in-the-signal-event-art.png`

### 7. The First Card Appears

Arc phase: Aftermath And Evidence

Narrative beat: A physical MythCards artifact appears near Orlov's coma bed. It is not a dream, not a transcript, and not a machine error. It can be held.

Why it matters: The Convergence can now make symbols into objects. The game itself enters the story as evidence, weapon, archive, and temptation.

Character pressure:
- Orlov's unconscious mind may be organizing the worlds.
- Karpova sees the first proof that the breach can produce valuable artifacts.
- Semyonov realizes evidence can multiply faster than paperwork can erase it.

Flavor line: "Dreams can be denied. Objects have weight."

Visual direction: hospital bed, Orlov unconscious, snow or frost near the pillow, first ornate card glowing with impossible materiality, officials outside the glass.

Mechanical identity: artifact clarity. Choose your Hero or one ally adjacent to your Hero. That character gains +1 RANGE on its next attack or AP ability this turn, and may remove 1 harmful temporary combat marker.

Asset: `assets/events/closed-city-the-first-card-appears-event-art.png`

### 8. Semyonov Orders Silence

Arc phase: Aftermath And Evidence

Narrative beat: Semyonov locks down the facility, confiscates statements, threatens witnesses, and orders silence. The command structure tries to make the event smaller than it is.

Why it matters: This is the political answer to the mystical breach. It shows that containment is not only physical; it is emotional, bureaucratic, and violent.

Character pressure:
- Semyonov believes secrecy is survival.
- Karpova believes secrecy is ownership.
- Nikolai, Anya, Elena's records, and the first card all threaten the official lie.

Flavor line: "The order was silence. The city kept dreaming anyway."

Visual direction: military lockdown corridor, sealed doors, confiscated files, soldiers in respirators, one witness hidden in shadow, signal glow leaking under a door.

Mechanical identity: suppression. Until the start of the active player's next turn, characters cannot use bonus AP gained from card effects.

## Set-Level Story Questions

- Was the Convergence an accident, invitation, or planned opening?
- Did Elena disappear because she crossed too far, because something took her, or because she chose to remain in the signal?
- Is Orlov the author of the first cards, the doorway they pass through, or only the first victim who can read them?
- How long has Karpova known that occult relics and technical systems could touch the same phenomenon?
- Can Semyonov's containment protect the city, or does it preserve the breach by trapping everyone with it?

## Set-Level Mechanical Notes

- Closed City events should emphasize risk, status transfer, placed objects, Leak markers, temporary movement through impossible routes, and suppression.
- Avoid giving multiple Closed City events top-deck viewing or manipulation. That lane should remain rare and mostly tied to specific relics such as Chintamani Fragment.
- Marked, Shield, +ATK, +MOVE, Slow, Pounce, and similar temporary effects should have visible board indicators in the digital prototype.
- Leak markers should feel like places where the board remembers the incident.
- Event cards should resolve quickly enough for prototype play while implying a larger narrative panel.
