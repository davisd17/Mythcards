Quick Rules

  1. Board is 7x7 with one center tile.
  2. Default movement is vertical/horizontal.
  3. Default attacks are vertical/horizontal line-of-sight.
  4. Movement, attack, ability, mount, and dismount each cost 1 action.
  5. Each character refreshes to 1 AP at the start of its side's turn.
  6. Tiger Pounce is activated only while the Tiger is not mounted, and marks its next attack as +2 ATK after moving.
  7. Use character indicators to track shields, temporary attack, movement, or Pounce reminders.
  8. Each player may have only 1 active relic.
  9. When drawing a relic with a relic already active, choose whether to replace it.
  10. Hero or Leader can mount an adjacent allied Mount as an action.
  11. Mounted pair uses rider HP/ATK/RANGE/abilities and Mount MOVE.
  12. If mounted rider HP hits 0, both rider and Mount are defeated.
  13. Reach the opponent's edge for Level 2.
  14. For Level 3, a Level 2 character must carry a Spirit Ember to the center square.
  15. Win by capturing the enemy Hero or defeating all enemy characters.
