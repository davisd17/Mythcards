## Sanctuary V1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: wide landscape environmental backdrop for the MythCards Godot tactical game, Flood Survivors starting-area theme. Reference is Queen Meret-Anu card artwork, use ONLY its architecture, materials and palette, no character. Create an enormous partly submerged Atlantean sanctuary beneath the earth, inhabited and maintained by survivors rather than abandoned ruins. Tall scalloped basalt pillars with shell and lotus carvings, ancient bronze celestial instruments, pale ivory mineral inlay, broad dry stone causeways dividing still black-water channels, distant arched openings into a turquoise underwater vault. Side alcoves hold sealed stone memory archives and warm pearl lamps. Architectural detail concentrated along left/right edges and upper corners, calm open central floor, scene must remain readable when game board and HUD cover the middle. Deep emerald and luminous restrained cyan water reflections balanced with pale stone, warm aged bronze, tiny muted coral accents, NOT an entirely blue scene or beige/brown scene. Tactile realistic illustrated dark-fantasy game environment, majestic, mysterious and resilient, no horror gore, no modern machinery, no temple religious emblems, no readable text, no characters, no portraits, no game board, no UI. Match reference's shell architecture and fine gold orbital details; no throne as central focal object. Opaque landscape background, rich inspectable surfaces not blurred or pitch black.

## Tide Stone V1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: square perfectly orthographic top-down battlefield surface for MythCards Flood Survivors. Single flat square ancient Atlantean stone dais, NO grid; engine draws 7x7 grid. Quiet playable central 83 percent is medium-light green-grey celadon stone with gentle ivory fossil-shell veins and extremely faint flowing water-caustic etching. Restrained long fine bronze inlay curves near margins, central area mostly open without any focal medallion. Clearly STONE AND PEARL, not concrete, not metal industrial floor. Perimeter outer 8.5 percent on each edge is a rich architectural frame of deep emerald carved basalt, antiqued bronze shell/lotus motifs, pale opalescent insets, narrow turquoise water channels, small muted coral stone accents, shell-rosette corners. The frame must be perfectly square and flat, straight edges, no perspective or cast shadows beyond image, so it can be nine-sliced into a game board edge. Material detail tactile realistic premium fantasy game art. Not entirely blue, not beige/brown, balanced celadon/ivory jade and bronze. Contrast central floor gently lighter than frame, suitable for tiny dressed humanoid sprites. NO characters, no objects blocking play, no readable text, no numbers, no letters, no checkerboard, no pre-drawn tiles, no dividing seams in the center, no central circle, no UI, no enormous cracks. Opaque square image edge to edge.
