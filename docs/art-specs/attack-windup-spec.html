Attack Wind-Up Frames

Six key poses of each character's basic attack, drawn in the existing board-figure style. The game blends them in sequence over 600 ms every time that character attacks. Revised after the designer requested a smoother six-image pilot.

Scope: 14 characters × 6 framesStart: 1 pilot characterFormat: 512 × 768 PNG, transparent
Start here

Pilot: Major Yuri Volkov

Please deliver Yuri's six frames first. We'll drop them into the game, check them on a phone-sized board, and send feedback before you start the other 13. Any problem with the spec gets fixed once, not fourteen times.

His attack is a ranged rifle shot (Containment Shot, range 2). The picture is his current figure: your frames should look like this same man in motion.

The sequence

Six poses, in this order

The game blends between your poses and adds small movement (a lean, a short step toward the target), so each frame should be a clear, held pose rather than an in-between. The widths below are proportional to how long each pose is on screen.

1 · Ready100 msLow combat ready, eyes on target; bridge from idle.
2 · Raise100 msWeapon rises through a clear intermediate pose.
3 · Brace130 msAnticipation: shoulder the rifle, bend knees and brace; other attacks coil or charge.
4 · Fire60 msRelease toward screen-right; muzzle flash, energy or melee strike.
5 · Recoil90 msAbsorb the shot or swing; distinct follow-through before lowering.
6 · Recover120 msSettle toward idle. Blend the transition back to default.

Size on screen: a figure is about 60–120 px tall on a phone. The pose has to read as a silhouette at that size: keep the limbs and weapon clear of the body, and push the wind-up further than feels natural at full size.

Technical

The canvas

Foot baseline, y = 756. The lowest pixel of the feet sits 12 px above the bottom edge in every frame. The game pins this line to the board tile, so a frame whose feet sit higher or lower makes the character jump.
Center line, x = 256. The body stays centered on the same point in all six frames. Only weapons and effects reach past it.
Safe area: 24 px from each side, 18 px from the top. Nothing, including effects, may touch the canvas edge.
Quick check: lay default.png over each frame at 50% opacity. The feet should line up and the body should be the same size.
RequirementDetails
FilePNG, RGBA, 512 × 768 px, transparent background. One file per pose. Please also send the layered source (PSD, Krita, or similar).
FacingThree-quarter view, attacking toward screen-right, like the current figures. The game mirrors the frames for attacks to the left, so avoid lettering or one-sided details that would look wrong flipped.
ScaleThe body matches default.png exactly. Do not crop, re-center, or rescale frames one by one.
StyleMatch the current figure: the same painted, semi-realistic rendering, outfit, palette, lighting direction, and level of detail.
No groundNo floor, base, ground shadow, or background. The game draws the base and shadow.
EffectsMuzzle flash, energy, water, or dust may be painted into the Strike frame (and lightly into Wind-up), attached to the figure and inside the safe area.
OutfitDefault outfit only. Zoya's Aurora Rite and Thalassa-Nekh's Drowned Seraph alternates are out of scope for now.

Sign-off

Acceptance checklist

  • Six PNGs per character, named as above, 512 × 768, transparent; total hold duration 600 ms.
  • Feet on y = 756 and the body centered on x = 256 in every frame; matches default.png at 50% overlay.
  • Nothing touches the canvas edge (24 px sides, 18 px top).
  • Attacks toward screen-right; nothing reads wrong when mirrored.
  • Wind-up reads clearly as a silhouette at 80 px tall.
  • Same outfit, palette, and rendering as the current figure; no ground or shadow.
  • Layered source file included.
